# Strategic military planning v1

This tranche replaces the normal ruler-facing troop-count control with persistent military intent.

Rulers choose a posture, war-planning target, minimum garrison floor, spending priority, desired preparation time, and assumptions about vassal/allied help. The military planner derives an authorised establishment and reports current personnel, garrison demand, desired field force, estimated enemy strength/uncertainty, and discounted expected support.

War invitations are courier messages rather than instant diplomacy. They carry a route, travel time, secrecy and requested contribution, can be intercepted on unsafe/contested land or hostile-patrolled seas, and only resolve when they arrive. Accepted invitations create a war commitment and put the recipient into war-preparation posture against the named enemy.

Coalition armies are not yet merged into one campaign object. The existing campaign engine still permits only one active attacker/defender pairing in a region, so this tranche prepares separate allied mobilisation without teleporting allied manpower into the ruler's army. Multi-army theatres belong with subregional control and amphibious logistics.
